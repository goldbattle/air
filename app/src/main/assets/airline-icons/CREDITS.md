# Airline icons

Airline logos and emblems are trademarks of their owners and are used here only to identify the airline.
Each icon is cropped from a Wikimedia Commons file (Commons' own PNG rendering), trimmed and padded to a
96x96 transparent PNG; LH and KL are recolored white to sit on the brand color.
Where Commons has only a wordmark, the icon is the wordmark (AS) or its first letters (B6, F9, NK, FR).
United's current globe is fair-use only, so UA uses the public-domain 1974 tulip U.
